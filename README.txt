# Ornique Jewelry — Free Website

A responsive, premium/modern static online jewelry shop for Ornique Jewelry, Bangladesh.

## Included
- Responsive homepage
- Product collection with categories
- Shopping bag/cart
- Instagram + Facebook links
- Your supplied Ornique logo
- Bangladesh Taka pricing
- Mobile-friendly design

## Important
The six product photos are demo images from Unsplash. Replace the image URLs in `index.html` with your real product photos before launch.

To change products, edit the `products` array near the bottom of `index.html`.

## Free publishing
You can publish this site for free with services such as GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Cloudflare Pages. A custom domain name is optional and usually costs extra.

## Contact / order flow
The "Order via Instagram" button currently opens @ornique.bd. You can later connect a WhatsApp number, Messenger, bKash/Nagad checkout, or a full payment gateway.
